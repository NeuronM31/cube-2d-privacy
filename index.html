Privacy Policy

We have no accounts and no server of our own, and we do not collect your personal data. The iPhone, iPad and Mac version contains no advertising and no analytics.

The free Android version shows a Google AdMob ad only when you choose to watch one. Google processes the data described in section 06 for this. With Premium there are no ads.

01Who is responsible

The app “M31 Edu Cube 2D” (identifier ch.m31.edu.cube2d) is published by Neuron M31 (Andrzej Węgrzyn). For privacy matters, write to the address given at the top of this page.

02Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files or location, and shows no system permission prompts. The Android version has ordinary permissions that are granted without asking: vibration, internet access, Google Play purchases and — for the Google ad module — the advertising ID. Details are in sections 04 and 06.

03Data on your device

The app stores on your device: the language you chose, your first name (if you enter it — used only for the greeting in the app), an installation ID (made from the language code and the time of the first start; it is shown under “About” so that you can quote it when you contact us), settings, the game in progress, your cube designs, the free-version limit counters and whether Premium is active. The app sends none of this anywhere, and we have no access to it.

If you have Android Backup (Auto Backup) enabled, Android may copy this data to your Google account; on iOS the same can happen through an iCloud backup of your device. That happens within Google’s or Apple’s services, under their privacy policies, and remains under your control — you can turn it off in your device settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes the stored data. You can also reset it by clearing the app’s data in system settings.

04In-app purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase made through the billing system of Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone, iPad, Mac). The store handles the entire purchase — the app never sees or stores card details, billing addresses or any other payment information. It only asks the store whether Premium belongs to the account signed in on the device and stores that answer locally. The stores send us sales reports (for example order number, date, country and price), which we need for accounting and refunds.

Premium bought in the App Store works on all your iPhone, iPad and Mac devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Purchases do not transfer between Google Play and the App Store.

The app connects to the internet for two purposes only: to handle purchases through the store and — only in the free Android version — to show an ad (section 06).

05Challenges

A challenge is a short code (16 characters, or 24 in free play; it starts with M31) that describes a scrambled cube: its size, colour arrangement, net and number of moves. It contains no name, no account, no device identifier and no free text. The app does not send codes to any server.

When you share a challenge, you choose how: with your device’s share function or by copying the code. The service you pick (a messenger, e-mail, etc.) handles the message under its own privacy policy.

You receive a challenge by typing the code or using “Paste from clipboard” — the app reads the clipboard only when you tap that button. Nothing is sent back to the sender.

06Advertising — free Android version only

When. The Google ad module runs only in the free Android version, after the trial period. During the trial, with Premium and in the iPhone, iPad and Mac version there are no ads.

How. It is a rewarded ad: it appears only after you tap “Watch an ad” and, once watched, unlocks extra features for 10 minutes. The app never shows ads on its own. So that the ad can start without waiting, the module loads it in advance — Google may therefore process data even if you never watch an ad.

Who. Google AdMob — Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere.

What data. Google may process:

  • your IP address, also to estimate your approximate location,
  • the advertising ID and the app set ID,
  • interactions with the app and the ad (app start, taps, video views),
  • diagnostic data (for example launch time and performance).

Purposes: showing and measuring ads, analytics and fraud prevention. The data is transmitted encrypted. From Google we receive only aggregated statistics (such as the number of ads shown), with no data that identifies you.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form appears before the first ad. You can change your choice at any time: Settings → “Ad privacy settings”. If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads. Google’s consent module stores your choice on the device.

No lock-out. If no ad can be shown (no consent, no connection, no ad available), the app shows a card about our app M31 Edu Arabic (section 07) and unlocks the features anyway.

Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in the Android settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Ad content is limited to Google’s “PG” maturity rating.

Legal basis (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection): your consent where it is required; otherwise our legitimate interest in financing the free version and in preventing fraud.

More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

07Promotion of our own app

When Google has no ad, the app shows a card about our app M31 Edu Arabic. “View in store” opens Google Play. The app passes no data on when doing so.

08What the app does not do

  • no user account, no sign-in,
  • collects no name, email address or phone number — your first name, if you enter it, stays on the device,
  • does not determine your location (only Google may estimate an approximate location from the IP address in the version with ads, section 06),
  • has no analytics, usage statistics or crash reporting of its own,
  • never shows ads on its own, and none at all on iPhone, iPad and Mac,
  • has no chat, no user-submitted content and no leaderboards,
  • sells no data and shares none with third parties; the only exception is the Google ad module in the free Android version (section 06).

09Children

M31 Edu Cube 2D is an educational puzzle. The free Android version, which can show an ad, is not directed at children under 13, and ad content is limited (section 06). The iPhone, iPad and Mac version contains no ads and collects no data. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Purchases are handled by the store, which applies its own parental controls.

10Your rights

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. You can withdraw your consent to ads at any time in Settings → “Ad privacy settings”.

We hold no personal data about you ourselves, so there is nothing we could disclose, correct or delete. Advertising data is processed by Google — you can manage it in My Ad Center. You control the data stored by the app through your device settings. If you have any privacy questions, write to the contact address.
