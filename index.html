Privacy Policy

This app collects no personal data, sends nothing to any server of ours, and contains no advertising or analytics. Everything created while using the app stays on your device.

01Who is responsible

The app “M31 Edu Cube 2D” (identifier ch.m31.edu.cube2d) is published by Neuron M31 (Andrzej Węgrzyn). For privacy matters, write to the address given at the top of this page.

02Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files or location. Details of its network use are in section 04.

03Progress and settings

Your progress (completed stages), settings, colour choices, move counters and challenge counters are stored on your device. We have no access to them.

If you have Android Backup (Auto Backup) enabled, Android may copy this data to your Google account; on iOS the same can happen through an iCloud backup of your device. That happens within Google’s or Apple’s services, under their privacy policies, and remains under your control — you can turn it off in your device settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes the stored data. You can also reset it by clearing the app’s data in system settings.

04In-app purchases

Paid features (Premium) are sold through the billing system of Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). The store handles the entire purchase process — the app never sees or stores card details, billing addresses or any other payment information. It receives only a confirmation that a purchase is active, which is stored locally in encrypted form.

The app uses an internet connection to handle purchases and to check for updates. It connects to the store’s services and nothing else.

05Challenges

A challenge is a short code (about 16 characters, starting with M31.) that describes a scrambled cube: its size, the colour arrangement and the number of moves. It contains no name, no account, no device identifier and no free text. The app does not send codes to any server.

When you share a challenge, you choose how: with your device’s share function or by copying the code. The service you pick (a messenger, e-mail, etc.) handles the message under its own privacy policy.

You receive a challenge by typing or pasting the code into the app. Nothing is sent back to the sender.

06What the app does not do

  • no user account, no sign-in,
  • collects no name, email address, phone number or advertising identifier,
  • does not determine or store your location,
  • uses no analytics, usage statistics or crash-reporting services,
  • shows no advertising,
  • has no chat, no user-submitted content and no leaderboards,
  • shares no data with third parties, because it gathers none.

07Children

M31 Edu Cube 2D is an educational puzzle for learners of all ages. It does not ask for or collect personal data from anyone, children included. It has no accounts, no advertising and no chat. Purchases are handled by the store, which applies its own parental controls.

08Your rights

Because we neither collect nor process your personal data, we hold no information that we could disclose, correct or delete on request. You control the data stored by the app yourself, through your device settings. If you have any privacy questions, write to the contact address.

09Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes in the future, we will update this page and change the “Valid from” date at the top. We recommend checking this page occasionally.
